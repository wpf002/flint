Flint

Flint is a personal assistant that runs on its owner's own Mac.

Flint answers its owner's questions, keeps track of what is coming up, and asks before it acts, unless its owner has let a task run on its own. It is a private, single-user project: one person installs it on their own Mac, and Flint keeps its data there.

Google Calendar

When its owner turns it on, Flint reads their Google Calendar with read-only access. It uses what it reads to remind its owner what is coming up, to answer their questions about their schedule, and, with their approval, to add people from their events as contacts. It never changes, creates or deletes events.

When the owner asks about their calendar in a chat answered by a cloud AI model, the details needed for the answer are sent to that model. The privacy policy explains exactly what Flint reads, where it keeps it, who it is shared with, and how to stop it.
